Free PDF Merge & Split

Combine PDFs into one file, or split one PDF into individual pages. Everything happens locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Drop PDF files here, or click to choose files
Add two or more PDFs. Drag list items to reorder is not supported yet — files merge in the order added.
    Drop a PDF here, or click to choose a file
    Splits every page into its own downloadable PDF.

      How this works: this page uses the open-source pdf-lib library, running entirely as JavaScript in your browser tab. Your PDF files are read locally and are never sent to any server.
